## Review instructions
1. Review the lesson notes, worked examples, practice questions, practical tasks, checklists and performance rubrics in the app.
2. Compare learning outcomes and activities with the official NERDC October 2025 SSS1 Digital Technologies curriculum. Do not treat the older 70-topic term/week map as proof of alignment.
3. Judge age appropriateness, technical accuracy, clarity, local relevance, safe practice, inclusion/accessibility and whether assessment evidence demonstrates the stated competency.
4. Distinguish physical practical work from paper/simulation alternatives; do not award equivalent physical-skill evidence for a paper simulation.
5. Record each correction with the lesson ID, evidence and a suggested correction/source. Mark uncertainty for verification rather than guessing.

## Rating scale
- **4 — Ready:** accurate, teachable and adequately assessed.
- **3 — Minor revision:** sound overall; small clarification needed.
- **2 — Major revision:** important content, sequence, example or assessment gap.
- **1 — Not ready:** materially inaccurate, unsafe, misleading or not teachable as written.
- **N/A — Not reviewed / not applicable.**
